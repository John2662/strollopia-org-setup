# Student Guide — LangWalk

LangWalk is a language learning app you use while walking around a city. Your teacher creates a route with stops, and at each stop you complete language activities — listening, speaking, writing, or answering questions. Stops unlock in order, so you work through the route step by step.

---

## Before you go out

### 1. Create your account

Your teacher or school administrator will give you a link to sign up. Once you have an account, log in at **langwalk.strollopia.com**.

### 2. Join your challenge

Your teacher will give you either:

- A **link** like `langwalk.strollopia.com/join/XK7R2M` — tap it and press **Join Challenge**, or
- A **code** (e.g. `XK7R2M`) — go to `langwalk.strollopia.com/join`, type the code, and press Continue.

Once joined, the challenge appears on your home screen under **My Challenges**.

### 3. Check your phone

- Make sure your phone's location is turned on (GPS) — you'll need it to navigate to each stop.
- Download any offline maps if you're going somewhere with patchy signal.
- Charge your battery before heading out.

---

## On the walk

### Finding your first stop

1. Open the app and tap your challenge.
2. You'll see a list of stops. The **first stop is already unlocked**. The others are locked until you complete the one before them.
3. Use your phone's maps app (Google Maps, Apple Maps, etc.) to navigate to the first stop — copy the address or drop a pin from the stop's location.

> There is no built-in turn-by-turn navigation — use your usual maps app alongside LangWalk.

### Completing a stop

When you arrive at a stop, tap it to expand it. You'll see the activities for that location.

**Activity types you might encounter:**

| Icon | Type | What you do |
|------|------|-------------|
| Check-in | Confirm you're there | Tap "Check in here" |
| Quiz | Multiple choice | Pick an answer, tap Submit |
| Listen | Audio clip + question | Listen, then answer the question |
| Write | Written response | Type your answer, tap Submit |
| Speak | Spoken prompt | Read the prompt aloud, then mark it done |
| Photo | Photograph something | Follow the instruction, submit |

Complete **all activities** at a stop to unlock the next one.

### Quizzes

- Tap an answer to select it (highlighted in orange).
- Tap **Submit** to lock in your answer.
- Green = correct, red = your wrong pick, faded = other options.
- An explanation may appear after you submit — read it.
- You can't re-attempt a submitted quiz.

### Write and Speak activities

These are reviewed by your teacher — there's no automatic right/wrong. Write your best attempt and submit. For Speak activities, say the prompt aloud to practise pronunciation, then submit.

### Check-in

Just confirms you've physically arrived at the location. Tap **Check in here**.

---

## When a stop is locked

A padlock icon means you haven't finished the previous stop yet. Go back and complete all activities there first.

---

## After the walk

Your progress is saved automatically. Your teacher can see which stops you've completed. For Speak and Write activities, expect feedback from your teacher separately (email, classroom, etc.).

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Can't see the challenge | Make sure you joined it — go to `/join` and enter the code again |
| App won't load | Check your internet connection; try refreshing the page |
| Stop won't unlock | Make sure every activity at the previous stop shows as submitted |
| Audio won't play | Check your phone volume and try a different browser |
| Submitted wrong quiz answer | Answers are final — let your teacher know if there was a problem |

---

## Tips for a better experience

- **Do a quick read-through** of the challenge on your home screen before heading out so you know how many stops there are and roughly how long it will take.
- **Walk the route with a partner** if possible — speaking activities are more natural with someone to practise with.
- **Screenshot or note the stop locations** before you leave if you're worried about connectivity.
- **Take your time at each stop** — the point is the language practice, not speed.
